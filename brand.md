# Brand Guidelines — For You, Always.

> "A memory to keep, a voice to remember."

## 1. Brand Essence

**For You, Always.** (internally known as **Voices**) is a digital curation platform designed to bridge the gap between ephemeral digital communication and lasting tactile memories. We provide a "Studio" for creators to build intimate, audio-visual gifts—"Memoir Stations"—that honor relationships through shared voice notes, photographs, and ambient atmosphere.

### Our Mission
To transform digital fragments into meaningful artifacts of affection.

---

## 2. Voice & Tone

Our communication is an extension of the gift-giving experience: **Intimate, Elegant, and Timeless.**

- **Intimate**: We speak with warmth and personal connection.
- **Elegant**: We use precise, minimal language. We prefer the quiet over the loud.
- **Timeless**: Our brand feels like a vintage heirloom found in a modern world.
- **Indonesian Fluency**: Much of our user-facing content is in Indonesian, maintaining a poetic and respectful tone (e.g., *"Ada hadiah spesial untukmu"*, *"Galeri Kenangan"*).

## 4. Design Principles

### 4.1 Mechanical Metaphor
The "Memoir Station" is not just a player; it is a machine.
- **Screws & Plates**: UI elements should feel "bolted" onto the surface.
- **The Crank**: Interaction is tactile. Users "crank" the handle to reveal memories.
- **The Lens**: Photos are viewed through a convex lens with glass reflections.

### 4.2 Analog Textures
We embrace the "perfectly imperfect."
- **Analog Noise**: Every surface has a subtle `0.08` opacity fractal noise.
- **Light Leaks**: Transitions and interactions (like cranking) trigger warm flickers.
- **Mesh Gradients**: Backgrounds are never flat; they use multiple radial gradients to create depth.

### 4.3 Glassmorphism
We use depth to create focus.
- **Backdrop Blur**: `20px` to `50px` blur on containers allows the mesh gradient to bleed through.
- **Saturate**: `180%` saturation on glass elements makes them feel vivid and premium.
